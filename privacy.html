Privacy Policy

Pre-launch draft. This policy must be finalized against the production backend, analytics, hosting, authentication providers and legal entity details before StudentHood accepts public accounts.

StudentHood will collect only the information needed to provide, secure and improve the service. Depending on the features a user chooses, this may include account details, profile information, content, messages, connection activity, device information and approximate or precise location where the user explicitly enables a location feature.

How information may be used

  • Provide accounts, profiles and StudentHood features.
  • Deliver Pings, Scenes, Pulse posts, Hangs, Crews and Gigs.
  • Protect users, prevent abuse and enforce rules.
  • Operate, troubleshoot and improve the product.
  • Meet legal obligations where applicable.

Messages

Ping is the messaging area of StudentHood. Production privacy terms will specify message storage, retention, security and any service providers used to deliver messaging.

Your choices

Account, privacy, location, notification, blocking and deletion controls will be documented here before launch.
