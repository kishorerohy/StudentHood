Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about StudentHood and how the platform is designed to work.

What is StudentHood?

StudentHood is an independent social platform built around everyday student life: people, conversations, moments, meetups, communities and opportunities.

Is StudentHood affiliated with any university?

No. StudentHood is independent and does not claim to be affiliated with, endorsed by, operated by, or partnered with any university.

What is Pulse?

Pulse is the everyday StudentHood feed for posts, updates and activity from people and the wider student community around you.

What are Scenes?

Scenes are StudentHood’s immersive photo and video format for sharing moments as they happen.

What are Peeps?

Peeps are the people you connect with on StudentHood.

What is Ping?

Ping is the dedicated messaging area. Direct messages, Ping requests, Hang chats and Crew chats belong inside Ping.

What are Drops?

Drops are non-message activity notifications, such as Peep activity, Scene interactions, Hangs, Crews, Gigs and other app events. Message alerts are kept inside Ping.

What are Hangs, Crews and Gigs?

Hangs are casual meetups and plans. Crews are smaller communities built around shared interests. Gigs are student-relevant opportunities such as work, projects and other useful opportunities.

How do I report a problem?

Use the Report a Problem route in Support. When live accounts are enabled, reporting controls will also be available from relevant content and profile menus inside StudentHood.

How can I contact StudentHood?

Use the Contact Us page for general enquiries and support-related questions.

Where can I read about privacy and safety?

See the Privacy Policy, Safety page, Community Guidelines and Terms of Service linked in the footer of every public StudentHood page.
