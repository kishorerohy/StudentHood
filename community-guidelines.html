Community Guidelines

StudentHood is intended for real student connection. Users should treat other people with respect and use the platform in ways that do not put others at risk.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, threats or targeted abuse.
  • Impersonation or deceptive identity practices.
  • Non-consensual intimate content or sexual exploitation.
  • Spam, scams, fraudulent Gigs or malicious links.
  • Content or conduct that facilitates violence or serious harm.

Enforcement

StudentHood may limit distribution, remove content, restrict features or suspend accounts when rules are violated. Final enforcement language will be aligned with the production moderation system before launch.
