Support

StudentHood Support will help with account access, privacy controls, reporting, safety, technical problems and product questions.

Help Centre

The Help Centre will include account setup, password recovery, profile controls, Ping, Drops, Scenes, Hangs, Crews, Gigs, reporting and blocking guidance.

Report a Problem

Once accounts are enabled, users will be able to report content and account issues from inside StudentHood. A web support route will also be provided for users who cannot access their account.
