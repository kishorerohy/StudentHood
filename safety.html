Safety

StudentHood is being designed with reporting, blocking, privacy controls and clear community standards as core product features.

Controls

  • Block or report another account.
  • Report Scenes, Pulse posts, Pings, Hangs, Crews and Gigs where applicable.
  • Control who can Ping you and interact with your content.
  • Keep message notifications inside Ping rather than mixing them into Drops.

Emergencies

StudentHood is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact the relevant local emergency service.
