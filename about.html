About Us

StudentHood is an independent social platform being built around everyday student life: people, conversations, moments, meetups, communities and opportunities.

What we are building

StudentHood is designed around Pulse, Discover, Scenes, Peeps, Ping, Hangs, Crews and Gigs. Our goal is to give student life a dedicated digital space without presenting StudentHood as affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by any university.

Our approach

We are designing StudentHood around authentic connection, clear safety controls and a simple separation between messaging and other activity. Direct messages and Ping requests belong in Ping, while Drops is reserved for non-message activity.
